About the Tribe Initiative

What you are being invited into

The Church Life Africa (CLA) Tribe Initiative is a structured network of four charism-based communities — Faith Explorers, Service Builders, Innovators Circle, and Culture Creators. Each tribe gathers members according to their dominant God-given gift and channels that gift into the concrete work of CLA's programmes: Bible in a Year, Exploring Catholicism, Theology for Service Leadership, Rural Evangelization, the Amen App, and more.

The Tribe Initiative is being formally relaunched in 2026 with a restructured operational model, a new handbook, and a renewed focus on measurable formation and outreach outcomes. Stewards and Co-Stewards are the most critical appointments in this relaunch. They set the culture, carry the vision, and hold the tribe together. Without strong leadership at the tribe level, the Initiative cannot fulfil its purpose.

This is not a ceremonial title. It is a calling to sacrificial, servant leadership within a Catholic lay missionary community.

2
The Two Roles

Steward and Co-Steward — how they differ

There are two distinct but inseparable roles. They operate as a pair. You are applying for one of them based on the tribe you are being considered for. Both roles are equally essential to the tribe's health.

Primary leader
The Steward
Vision · Representation · Accountability
Holds the tribe's vision and sets its spiritual tone
Represents the tribe externally to CLA leadership
Chairs the monthly tribe meeting
Sits on the cross-tribe Tribe Council
Submits monthly and quarterly reports
First point of accountability for tribe health
Operational leader
The Co-Steward
Operations · Member Care · Execution
Manages day-to-day tribe operations and calendar
Leads member onboarding, care, and retention
Oversees the tribe's Support Team of three
Tracks project timelines and deliverables
Maintains the tribe's documentation repository
Fully represents the tribe in the Steward's absence

A note on the Steward–Co-Steward relationship

These two roles are designed to complement each other, not to compete. The Steward carries strategic and representational responsibility; the Co-Steward carries operational and relational responsibility. They must meet briefly at least once a week, support each other unconditionally, and cover each other's absence without friction. CLA will not appoint a Steward and Co-Steward who do not agree to work in this spirit.

Who We Are Looking For

The person we need

We are not looking for the most knowledgeable person, the most popular person, or the most experienced person. We are looking for the right person — someone whose character, charism, and current life season align with what this role genuinely requires.

Essential Character

  • Practising Catholic with a living, growing faith
  • Servant heart — genuinely motivated by the good of others, not personal profile
  • Integrity — does what they say; owns mistakes without deflection
  • Reliability — consistently follows through on commitments
  • Humility — able to receive feedback, be corrected, and give credit generously
  • Charity — handles conflict without bitterness or divisiveness

Essential Competencies

  • Communication — clear, warm, and consistent in written and verbal communication
  • Organisation — can manage a calendar, track tasks, and meet deadlines
  • Initiative — acts without waiting to be told; spots needs and responds
  • People awareness — notices when members are struggling, disengaging, or thriving
  • Digital literacy — comfortable with Google Meet, WhatsApp, Google Forms, and basic Drive

Steward-Specific Requirements

  • Has demonstrated leadership in a church, community, or professional setting
  • Comfortable speaking to a group and presenting publicly (virtual or in-person)
  • Can write a clear, structured report without extended assistance
  • Able to make decisions under pressure and own the outcome
  • Willing to hold members accountable — with kindness, but without avoidance

Co-Steward-Specific Requirements

  • Strong operational instincts — naturally notices what is not working and fixes it
  • Pastoral sensitivity — able to check in on members in a way that feels genuine
  • Detail-oriented — keeps records, tracks attendance, maintains documentation
  • Supports the Steward's vision without undermining or bypassing it
  • Prepared to lead fully if the Steward is unavailable for any period

Charism Alignment

  • The Steward and Co-Steward must be genuinely aligned with the charism of the tribe they lead. A Steward of Service Builders must be moved by mercy and practical charity. A Steward of Faith Explorers must be a person of prayer and formation. A Steward of Innovators Circle must genuinely think in terms of systems and solutions. A Steward of Culture Creators must have a living relationship with beauty, story, and expression.
  • Applicants who have completed the CLA Charism Discernment assessment and been assigned to a different tribe from the one they are applying to lead will be asked to explain their reasoning in the interview.

This role is not for you if…

  • You are looking for a title rather than a responsibility
  • Your current season of life does not allow you to honour the time commitment consistently
  • You are unwilling to submit regular reports and be held accountable to deadlines
  • You have a history of withdrawing from commitments when they become difficult
  • You cannot work in submission to CLA leadership and its established programmes
  • You are not currently in good standing as a practising Catholic
  • You are applying with the expectation of financial compensation — there is none

Term & Tenure

How long is the appointment?

Initial Term

  • Each Steward and Co-Steward serves an initial term of 12 months, aligned with CLA's operational year
  • The term begins at the formal relaunch date and ends at the Annual Tribe Review
  • No appointment is confirmed until a signed Letter of Acceptance has been received by CLA

Renewal

  • A Steward or Co-Steward may serve a maximum of two consecutive terms (24 months)
  • Renewal is not automatic — it is based on performance, reporting consistency, and mutual agreement
  • After two terms, a minimum one-year rest period is required before reappointment

Early Departure

  • A minimum of 30 days' written notice is required if you need to vacate the role early
  • You are expected to complete a full structured handover to your successor
  • Leaving without notice or a handover is treated as a serious breach of commitment

Succession

  • The Co-Steward is the preferred first candidate for Steward succession when a vacancy arises
  • Outgoing Stewards are expected to identify and recommend a successor in consultation with CLA
  • All succession decisions are confirmed by CLA leadership

Exploring Catholicism Mission Leader Programme — Mandatory Enrolment

All Stewards and Co-Stewards must be active participants in a current cohort of the Exploring Catholicism (EC) Mission Leader programme at the time of their appointment and throughout their term. If you are unsure of your status, confirm with the CLA Programme Director before submitting your application.

The application process is designed to be clear and respectful of your time. There are five steps. Each step serves a purpose — none of them is bureaucratic formality.

Before filling out any form, read every section of this document carefully. Do not apply if you have not fully assessed the requirements against your current season of life. A half-committed leader causes more harm than no leader.
Before applying
2

How We Select

Selection criteria

The selection panel evaluates applicants across five dimensions. These are assessed through the application form, the written vision statement, and the interview.

1. Character & Faith

  • Active, practising Catholic life
  • Evidence of integrity and follow-through in past commitments
  • Humility and openness to accountability
  • Ability to handle conflict charitably

2. Charism Fit

  • Clear alignment with the tribe's charism identity
  • Personal history of expressing that charism in service
  • Ability to articulate the tribe's charism in their own words

3. Capacity & Availability

  • Current life season genuinely allows the time commitment
  • No competing obligations that would routinely conflict with scheduled commitments
  • Stable enough personally to show up consistently

4. Leadership Competence

  • Past experience leading a team, group, or community
  • Communication and reporting ability
  • Problem-solving instinct and initiative
  • Ability to develop others, not just manage tasks

5. Vision & Alignment with CLA

Applicants must demonstrate a clear understanding of CLA's mission, the role of the Tribe Initiative within that mission, and a compelling personal vision for what their tribe can contribute in the coming year. Vague or generic answers in the vision statement will significantly reduce a candidate's prospects. The best applicants will have read the Tribe Handbook, understand the programmes the tribe supports, and can articulate specifically — not generally — what they want to build.

A word before you apply

We ask every applicant to sit quietly with this question before submitting anything: "Is this a calling, or is this convenience?" We are not looking for volunteers who have spare time. We are looking for leaders who sense a genuine call to steward a community of people using their God-given gifts for the mission of the Church. If that is you — even imperfectly, even nervously — we want to hear from you. If you are unsure, speak with your parish priest or a trusted Catholic mentor before applying. This role deserves that level of discernment.
