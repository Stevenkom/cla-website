Nnadozie Onyekuru

Programs Manager

Biography

Nnadozie serves as Programs Manager for Church Life Africa. Through this role, he liaises with core team members to curate signature programs such as Theology for Service Leadership and Exploring Catholicism. A mission-driven professional, Nnadozie comes to CLA with a sense of gratitude for his background in Catholic education, which he hopes to draw from, to drive reflective enthusiasm for faith, learning, and service among participants of CLA’s programs. After studying engineering at the University of Maiduguri where he was active in the local chaplaincy, he received fundamental philosophical and theological formation in the liberal arts B.A. program of Thomas Aquinas College, California. He also received a Master of Global Affairs from the University of Notre Dame where he explored Catholic Social Teaching’s concept of integral human development.
