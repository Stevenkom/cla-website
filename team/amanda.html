Biography

Amanda Hange Wuese is a driven and passionate individual with a strong foundation in faith, entrepreneurship, and community service. A graduate of Religious Studies, she is an entrepreneur who has successfully launched a startup business and is a certified professional makeup artist.

She holds various leadership roles in her community, including serving as a co-steward for the Service Builders Tribe of Church Life Africa. She is dedicated to making a positive impact and inspiring others through her work in digital communications and community engagement.
