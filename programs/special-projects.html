Special Programs & Initiatives

Church Life Africa expands the frontiers of faith through strategic partnerships and specialized programs designed for spiritual renewal and communal empowerment.

Strategic Partnerships

Grant Award 2025

Vital Worship Grant

CLA wins Calvin Institute’s 2025 Vital Worship Grant. In partnership with renowned Catholic choreographer and adjunct professor of dance at the University of Notre Dame, Abigail Zimmern, CLA piloted the “Dance as Prayer” project at its 2025 Summer Conference.

Economic Empowerment

Entrepreneurship Training

In a strategic alliance with Africa2100, CLA is providing comprehensive entrepreneurship training to young Catholics in Nigeria, merging business ethics with Catholic social principles to foster local economic growth.

Future Horizons

In Planning Phase

Pilgrimages & Retreats

We envision curated spiritual pilgrimages to sacred sites in Africa and around the world including Our Lady of Kibeho Apparition Site in Rwanda, and the Site of the Ugandan Martyrs, Holy Land, The Eternal City, etc.

Our new home at the Gaudium et Spes Institute Abuja, will also offer opportunities for individual and group retreats.
In Design Phase

Field Research

There are countless areas in the African Religious and Cultural space that are yet to be explored. In partnership with Academic and Research Institutions, CLA will work to promote efforts in gathering and deploying rich, reliable grassroot data through our team of researchers, data collectors and translators.
