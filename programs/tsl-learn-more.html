Theology for Service Leadership
Post-Graduate Certificate

Theology for Service Leadership (TSL) is a two-year postgraduate certificate program specially designed for lay Africans who are interested in delving into the foundational truths of the Christian faith and their practical implications. Through study, reflection, and application of knowledge, the program will train students to better articulate, live, and share their faith as witnesses to the Gospel.

With Catholic theological education as its center of gravity, the TSL program aims to equip students with requisite knowledge, skills, and experience for service leadership in the Church and society.

By the end of the program, students will:

  • Demonstrate appreciable competence in foundational Christian theology.
  • Be prepared to pursue further theological studies up to master’s and doctorate degrees.
  • Meet ecclesiastical certification standards for service as lay ecclesial minister or Catholic educator.
  • Develop solid ethical foundations and leadership capacities for professional, business, or vocational enterprises.

Program Curriculum

The TSL program curriculum comprises core courses in Scripture, Doctrine, Catholic Social Teaching, and African Christian Imagination, and area-elective courses in Pastoral Ministry, Public Engagement, and Entrepreneurship.

The program also includes a robust internship/mentorship placement where students learn to “give theology walking shoes.”

Guidelines

To graduate, students must complete eight core theology courses and four track-specific courses, making a total of twelve courses. The courses are a combination of pre-recorded self-paced classes, synchronous virtual classes, and intensive weeklong in-person classes offered during the summer.

Once you are admitted and matriculated into the TSL program, you will normally have two years to complete the required courses and present your final capstone project. For a justifiable reason, and with the prior approval of the program director, a student may be allowed up to three years to complete the program.
