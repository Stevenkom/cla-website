Service Builders

The Service Builders are the hands and feet of the mission—focused on practical, community-based expressions of faith. Their work is rooted in charity, presence, and direct service to people, especially the vulnerable.

About Service Builders

Service Builders operate on the conviction that faith is made visible through action. This tribe focuses on the practical needs of the Church and the community, transforming theological principles into tangible support.

From coordinating rural outreach logistics to leading local works of mercy, these members ensure that the love of Christ is felt in a physical and immediate way by those we serve.

Correctional Centre Visit

As part of our commitment to the corporal works of mercy, the Service Builders conducted a focused outreach to Kuje Prison. The team provided spiritual encouragement, shared meals, and essential supplies, witnessing to the restorative power of the Gospel behind bars.

Project Highlight

Elderly Visitation Initiative

Lokoja • Kaduna • Abuja • Anambra

The Service Builders Tribe coordinates a multi-state ministry of accompaniment, visiting vulnerable elderly community members to provide spiritual presence and practical support. By bridging the gap between generations, this project ensures that the wisdom of our elders is honored through consistent Christian fellowship and pastoral care.
