About the Tribe

The Faith Explorers tribes champions CLA's Faith formation, Catechetical, Scriptural and Prayer efforts. By diving deep into the treasures of sacrad scripture, Tradition, the lives of Saints, and Catholic Spirituality, By diving deep into Sacred Scripture and the Tradition of the Church, members equip themselves to be articulate witnesses of the Gospel in the modern world.

By carying the Gospel message to various communities, members inspire deeper faith response from Catholics and Non-Catholics alike, so that the truth of the Gospel is better understood, lived, and handed on.

Theological Resource Initiative

Catechism & Sacramental Distribution

Lafia Diocese • Abuja Diocese

The Faith Explorers successfully coordinated the distribution of essential theological materials to catechumens across two key regions. This initiative focuses on equipping new believers with the foundational tools of the faith, fostering both intellectual understanding and a deeper devotional life.

30 Catechism Booklets
50 Sacred Rosaries

Primary Sites: Holy Ghost Quasi Mission (Nyanya Gwandara) & Christ the King Catholic Church (Kurudu).
