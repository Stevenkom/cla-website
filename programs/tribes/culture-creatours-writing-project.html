Project Highlight

The Sacred Pen Workshop

Exploring the African Christian Imagination through Narrative

Writing as Evangelization

Culture Creators believe that every story told is an opportunity to witness to the Truth. During this virtual residency, tribe members gathered from across the continent to refine their craft in theological storytelling and creative non-fiction.

The project focused on "The Sacred Pen"—an initiative designed to help young African writers translate their faith experiences into compelling literature that speaks to both the Church and the modern world.

Workshop Details
  • Format: Virtual Masterclass
  • Duration: 4 Intensive Sessions
  • Focus: Narrative Theology
  • Outcome: Collaborative Essay Collection
