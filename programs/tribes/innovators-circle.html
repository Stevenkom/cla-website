About the Tribe

The Innovators Circle believes that modern technology and strategic collaboration are pivotal to the Church’s future in Africa. Driven by the spirit of the New Evangelization, this tribes explores new methods and new expressions for promoting the Gospel with renewed ardor.

We are dedicated to investing intentionally in digital frontiers, nurturing the seeds of hope for a flourishing, connected Church that transcends geographical barriers.
