How to Apply

  • • The program is specifically for lay African Catholics. Religious sisters who have proven records of active engagement with CLA may be considered. The clergy are not eligible for this program.
  • • Applicants must be practicing Catholics who demonstrate a strong commitment to deepening their theological formation and applying it to leadership and service within the Church and society.
  • • Applicants must have a bachelor’s degree or its equivalent in any discipline or can demonstrate significant knowledge and competence from personal study and work experience.

Application Requirements

To apply for the TSL program, prospective participants must submit the following:

• Online Application: Prospective participants must begin the process by submitting A COMPLETED ONLINE APPLICATION FORM → which serves as the primary record for your candidacy and eligibility review.

• Personal Statement: A 750-1000-word essay describing three things: (a) applicant’s academic background, faith formation, and leadership experience, (b) What books, other learning resources, and experiences have shaped their faith and life as Catholic, and (c) how the program aligns with their personal and vocational aspiration, and what concrete or potential areas they hope to serve or strengthen their service of the Church and society

• Curriculum Vitae (CV): A 2-3-page CV outlining academic background/qualifications, professional experience, leadership roles, relevant service activities, and other notable skills.

• Recommendation Letters: Request and upload two letters: (a) from a Catholic priest (or religious superior) who can attest to the applicant’s good standing as a practicing Catholic with demonstrable contribution to their parish and/or community (b) from a respectable lay mentor who can attest to the applicant’s personal, academic, and professional integrity, and their potential for becoming a transformative leader in the Church and society. These letters must reflect the recommenders’ personal and honest testimony of the applicant’s suitability for the TSL program.

Shortlisted candidates may be invited for a virtual conversation with the admissions committee. Final selection will be communicated within two weeks of this virtual meeting.
