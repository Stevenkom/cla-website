Official Call for Applications

CLA Summer Conference 2026

July 27 – August 1, 2026 | CSN Abuja, Nigeria
"The New Evangelization and the Future of Global Catholicism"

OPEN TO LAY CATHOLICS Church Life Africa (CLA) invites applications from lay Catholics for participation in the CLA 2026 Conference, scheduled to hold in July 2026. Applications must be submitted via the official CLA application form on or before the announced deadline.

1. Background & Vision

The CLA Summer Conference series is a formation hub bringing together Catholics from across Africa for a weeklong engagement with scholars, creatives, and ecclesiastical leaders from around the world. The conference integrates the Catholic intellectual life with pastoral, liturgical, and social engagement within the lived experience of the Church. The 2026 edition continues CLA's mission of investing in the theological formation of Africa’s vibrant Catholics who will become mission leaders to help Catholics to understand, live, and share their faith. This year, we will be reflecting on Pope St. John Paul II’s call for a New Evangelization - which invites Christians to proclaim the timeless message of Christ with “new method, new ardor, and new expression.”

3.Elegibility

Applications are open to lay Catholics who demonstrate:
Commitment to theological formation and Church life
Active leadership, pastoral involvement, or Catholic social engagement
Willingness to participate in CLA's formation and outreach programs beyond the conference
CLA intentionally reserves 40% of lay participation slots for new conference participants each year, ensuring ongoing renewal and broader inclusion.
