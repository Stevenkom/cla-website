CLA Impact Awards

Launched in August 2023, the CLA Impact Awards is an annual grant program intended as a mechanism for identifying and supporting impact-driven projects by CLA tribes whose works will make a difference in their communities. Suggested areas of focus include, but are not limited to, catechesis and liturgy, music and literature, art and culture, media and technology, social intervention and community-building, and Catholic social teaching. To ensure success, our monitoring and evaluation team tracks project progress through site visit, regular communication with grantees, and advice where needed.

The CLA Impact Awards and the TSL internship program are our strategic mechanisms for identifying and providing homegrown solutions to challenges of the local church and community. Over time, we will have developed a comprehensive database of proposed and implemented projects reflecting local challenges and strategic approaches to tackling them.

Impact Award Kickoff

On October 1st, 2023, the Church Life Africa Impact Award started accepting public applications. This distinguished award acknowledges the outstanding contributions of individuals and organizations to the community through their church work.

Application Ended

The Church Life Impact Award 2024 application period lasted for one month, from October 1st to October 31st, 2023. This prestigious award recognizes individuals and groups that have made a significant impact in their communities through outreach programs and initiatives.
