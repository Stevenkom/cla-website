QUARTERLY SERIES

Exploring Catholicism

Critical Conversations on Faith

This quarterly series brings together focus-groups of vibrant Catholics for critical conversations on the Catholic faith and life. Guided by seasoned scholars, participants meet for seven weeks every quarter to engage with topics of local and global importance to the life of the Church including theology, politics, public affairs, arts, science, and more.

In this program, participants cultivate the discipline of paying attention to, questioning humbly, and better understanding complex topics in the Church and world. Participants who attend and satisfactorily complete an entire year of Exploring Catholicism will be offered the opportunity for higher service in the CLA community.
