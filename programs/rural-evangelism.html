The Rural Evangelization Initiative

Currently in development, this program is dedicated to the faith formation of non-English speaking Catholics through the translation of resources into local heart languages, rural community outreach, and social impact programs for the poor and those on the margins of society.

In partnership with local Dioceses CLA takes up the challenging but life-giving mission of ministering to Christians in persecuted communities, Internally Displaced Persons (IDP) camps, and areas affected by natural disasters in Nigeria.

Proposed Areas of Focus

  • Translations of Catechetical and Liturgical materials into major African languages.
  • Development of new contents on Formed and Amen-App from local African contexts.
  • Adapting Local Songs, Prayers and arts for Theological Enrichment.
  • Fostering Solidarity with Catholics in rural areas through shared Prayers and mutual encouragement.
  • Deploying our mission leaders and Service Builders to rural communities for Evangelization.

Launching January 2027
