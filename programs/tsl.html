Theology for Service Leadership (TSL)

Theology for Service Leadership (TSL) is a two-year graduate certificate program specially designed for lay Africans who are interested in delving into the foundational truths of the Christian faith, and its applicability to various spheres of life. Through study, reflection, and application of knowledge, the program will train students to better articulate, live, and share their faith as witnesses to the Gospel.

With Catholic theological education as its center of gravity, the TSL program aims to equip students with requisite knowledge, skills, and experience for service leadership in the Church and society. By the end of the program, students will:

  • • Demonstrate appreciable competence in foundational Christian theology.
  • • Be prepared to pursue further theological studies up to master’s degrees.
  • • Meet ecclesiastical certification standards for service as lay ecclesial ministers or Catholic educators.
  • • Develop a solid foundation for leadership in professional, business, or vocational careers.
