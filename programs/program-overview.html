Program Overview

Once accepted into the TSL program, students will normally have two years to complete the required course work and present a final capstone project. For a justifiable reason, and with the prior approval of the program director, a student may be allowed up to three years to complete the program.

To complete the program, students must complete six core theology courses and four track-specific electives, making a total of ten courses. Eight of these courses are pre-recorded or real-time lectures offered synchronously, while two are intensive weeklong classes offered in person during the summer.

The program director will provide assistance/guidance with applications and onboarding process, scheduling courses, internship/mentorship, and other program completion requirements. A member of faculty will be available to provide assistance related to course content, assignments, exams, etc.

Curriculum

The TSL curriculum comprises core courses in Scripture, Doctrine, Catholic Social Teaching, and Global Catholicism, including track-specific courses in either Pastoral Theology or Public Theology. As an integral part of the program, students will be assigned to internship placements where they will learn to apply theological principles to real life contexts – “giving theology walking shoes.” Sample Courses include Introduction to Theology, Introduction to Scripture, Catholic Social Teaching, Global Catholicism and the Mission of the Church, The New Evangelization, Art of Catechesis etc.

Typical Course Schedule

  • • Summer: weeklong intensive in-person course
  • • Fall: 12 weeks hybrid synchronous course
  • • Spring: 12 weeks hybrid synchronous course
  • • Typically 25-30 students per cohort
  • • All lectures and course materials are available on Populi
  • • Supplemental readings are provided to encourage further exploration of topics – internet links are also provided for all course materials
  • • Facilitator-moderated Zoom sessions with students in the course.
  • • Assignment and Exam requirements TBD

Participation Requirements

  • • View or read the lecture for each unit.
  • • Read assigned texts; keep notes, questions, and comments for class discussion.
  • • Complete quizzes in each unit.
  • • Participate actively in all scheduled Zoom sessions throughout the course – except for excused absences
  • • Complete the course assignments/exams and course evaluation.

Time Expectations

12-15 hours per week, depending on your learning style and schedule.

Certificate of Completion & PLA Privilege

Upon satisfying all requirements for the successful completion of the program, each graduating student is awarded the Certificate in Theology of Service Leadership from the Augustine Institute Graduate School of Theology. As part of their application process, students who wish to apply for a master’s in theology at the Augustine Institute may take a Prior Learning Assessment (PLA) test for possible transfer of credit from courses completed through the TSL program.

Exit Talents

Depending on good standing and satisfactory completion of the program, and subject to the prudential judgement of the academic committee, graduating students may receive exit talents in the form of grants or career opportunities to advance cohort-initiated projects in service to the Church and society.

Program Cost: $2,000 (Two Years)

There is no application fee, and all accepted applicants will receive full scholarships for the duration of the program.
