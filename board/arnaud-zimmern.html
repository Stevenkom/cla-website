Dr. Arnaud Zimmern

Research Analyst specializing in International Fundraising

Biography

Dr. Arnaud Zimmern is a research analyst at the University of Notre Dame, specializing in international fundraising. He holds a doctorate in English Literature with a minor in the History and Philosophy of Science from Notre Dame, along with undergraduate degrees in Pure Mathematics, English, and World Languages from Southern Methodist University. He studied Christian Anthropology and Theology at the Institut Philanthropos in Fribourg, Switzerland. He is a former research fellow of the Consortium for the History of Science, Technology, and Medicine, a former Responsible Innovation fellow of the ND Tech Ethics Center, a past postdoctoral fellow of the Navari Family Center for Digital Scholarship, and former member of a global team of researchers at Notre Dame, Oxford, and Cambridge awarded a joint NEH/AHRC New Directions for Digital Scholarship in Cultural Institutions grant in 2022. He set aside academic pursuits in 2023 to dedicate himself to advancing data-driven methods for global academic fundraising. He now has the honor of serving on the advisory board of St. Joseph Catholic Church in Mishawaka, Indiana and as the director of the Notre Dame French Choir. Immensely honored to be on the CLA board, he is especially grateful for the opportunity to enhance his Igbo, Yoruba, devotion to God, and spice tolerance (the latter two may go hand-in-hand, depending on the chili pepper).
