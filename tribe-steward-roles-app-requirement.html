1 About the Tribe Initiative

The Church Life Africa (CLA) Tribe Initiative is a structured network of four charism-based communities — Faith Explorers, Service Builders, Innovators Circle, and Culture Creators. Each tribe gathers members according to their dominant God-given gift and channels that gift into the concrete work of CLA's programmes: Bible in a Year, Exploring Catholicism, Theology for Service Leadership, Rural Evangelization, the Amen App, and more.

The Tribe Initiative is being formally relaunched in 2026 with a restructured operational model, a new handbook, and a renewed focus on measurable formation and outreach outcomes. Stewards and Co-Stewards are the most critical appointments in this relaunch. They set the culture, carry the vision, and hold the tribe together. Without strong leadership at the tribe level, the Initiative cannot fulfil its purpose.

2 Steward and Co-Steward — how they differ

There are two distinct but inseparable roles. They operate as a pair. You are applying for one of them based on the tribe you are being considered for. Both roles are equally essential to the tribe's health.

The Steward

  • Holds the tribe's vision and sets its spiritual tone
  • Represents the tribe externally to CLA leadership
  • Chairs the monthly tribe meeting
  • Sits on the cross-tribe Tribe Council
  • Submits monthly and quarterly reports
  • First point of accountability for tribe health

The Co-Steward

  • Manages day-to-day tribe operations and calendar
  • Leads member onboarding, care, and retention
  • Oversees the tribe's Support Team of three
  • Tracks project timelines and deliverables
  • Maintains the tribe's documentation repository
  • Fully represents the tribe in the Steward's absence
