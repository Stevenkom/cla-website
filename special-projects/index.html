Strategic Partnership

Church Life Africa & Africa 2100

Forming Faith-Driven Entrepreneurs for the Service of Church and Society

Church Life Africa is partnering with Africa 2100 to equip young African Catholics with the entrepreneurial skills, mindset, and formation needed to transform their communities through dignified work and faith-driven leadership.

Addressing the Gap

Across Africa, millions of young people face limited access to employment and entrepreneurial infrastructure. At the same time, many Catholic youth desire to serve the Church and society meaningfully but lack the tools, guidance, and confidence to translate their vocation into sustainable economic action. This partnership responds directly to that gap.

The program combines practical business training with faith-based formation, grounded in Catholic social teaching, to help participants discern, develop, and launch ventures that serve real community needs.

"This pilot is delivered through a structured 12–16 week formation journey, guiding participants from idea development and validation to venture launch."

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Who is the program designed for?

The program is designed for young African Catholics who are interested in entrepreneurship or developing skills for economic impact.

2. Who can apply for the program?

Application to the program is strictly by invitation from Church Life Africa.

3. Do I need to have a business idea before joining?

No. The program is designed to guide you from early-stage idea discovery through validation and launch. If you already have an idea, you will refine and strengthen it. If not, you will be supported to develop one.

4. How long will the program last?

The program will run for 12 to 16 weeks.

5. Is the program online or in-person?

The training is primarily delivered through a digital platform, making it accessible from anywhere. There may also be a physical training component towards the end of the program.

6. What is expected of participants?

  • Actively engage with the learning platform
  • Complete course modules and assignments
  • Participate consistently throughout the program
  • Apply what they learn to real-life ideas or ventures

7. Will I receive funding for my business?

The program focuses on training, formation, and venture readiness. Direct funding after the program is not guaranteed.

8. How does this program connect with my faith?

This program is deeply rooted in Catholic values. It helps you see entrepreneurship not just as a career path but as a vocation, an opportunity to serve others and contribute to the mission of the Church.

9. Is there a cost to participate?

Participation in the program is free as all accepted applicants will receive full scholarships from Church Life Africa for the duration of the program.
