ABOUT US

Mobilizing African Catholics for the New Evangelization

As the fastest-growing Catholic population in the world, Africa continues to inspire hope for the future of the Church both in terms of numbers and commitment to the faith. Nigeria is at the center of this Christian vitality with 94% of its 35 million Catholics going to Mass every Sunday, and priestly vocations on a steady increase. However, this enthusiasm for the faith is not matched by intentional catechesis and ongoing formation of the laity. To survive the complex challenges of today’s world, the Church in Africa must be supported to consolidate its Catholic intellectual and moral foundation, so that the message of the Gospel can be better understood, preserved, and proclaimed with “new ardor, new method, and new expression. Church Life Africa exists to respond to this call for a New Evangelization.

CLA began operations in 2022 with the aim of investing in the future of Catholicism by training and mobilizing lay Catholics as theologians, catechists, and mission leaders for the New Evangelization. It accomplishes this through a variety of programs including annual formation conferences, a post-graduate certification in theology, Church Life impact projects, and cluster-based study groups. Church Life Africa was incorporated on June 28, 2024, as a domestic nonprofit corporation (501c3) in the State of Indiana, and granted the tax-exempt status by the United States Internal Revenue Service. With its principal activities in Africa, Church Life Africa (CLA) serves as the advisory, fundraising, and audit arm of Church Life Theology Initiative (CLTI), a registered NGO in Nigeria which oversees CLA’s operations, human resources, programs, and capital needs across Africa. Although distinct in their legal forms, both organizations maintain a unique operational partnership. 99% of funds raised for CLA’s mission are directly transferred to CLTI for operating and capital needs, and a consolidated board exercises oversight to ensure accountability

Strategic Partnerships

Our journey of formation and mission advancement has been blessed by strategic partnerships that continue to shape our work. We are especially grateful for the foundational support received from the University of Notre Dame’s de Nicola Center for Ethics and Culture, McGrath Institute for Life, Kellogg Institute for International Studies, and Department of Theology. Their early investment helped water the ground for CLA’s formative vision and long-term impact.

Building on that legacy, we are now honored to enter into substantial partnership with the Augustine Institute, one of America's foremost apostolates for lay theological mission. Through a multi-year partnership, the Augustine Institute and Church Life Africa are advancing the mission of the New Evangelization in Africa – forming African Catholics to understand, live, and share their faith. Church Life Africa’s institutional base is at the Augustine Institute in Florissant, Missouri, USA,

Consolidating its homebase within the Archdiocese of Abuja, CLA has secured a five-year lease of the penthouse floor at the Gaudium et Spes Institute in Asokoro, Abuja, including office and meeting rooms, library and education spaces, chapel, auditorium, and modest lodging.

Encouraged by these partnerships, and guided by the Holy Spirit, we are poised to advance the Church's mission, shaping a new generation of African Catholics equipped to lead the Church into the coming global era.

OUR VISION

Investing in the Future of the Global Church

The growth and vitality of Christianity in Africa signals great hope for the future of the Church. We seek to invest intentionally in Africa’s lay theological mission - forming the next generation of faithful Catholics for the global Church and society.

OUR MISSION

Forming African Catholics for the New Evangelization

Inspired by the exhortation of St. Peter, “Always be ready to give an explanation for your hope [in Christ]” (1 Peter 3:15), our mission is to mobilize African Catholics for the New Evangelization through critical theological education, faithful catechetical formation, and transformative pastoral leadership.

Mission & Partnership

Our primary goal is to close the formation gap for the laity in Africa by training and deploying theologians, catechists, and mission leaders for the New Evangelization, thereby building up the Body of Christ on the continent.

The Augustine Institute is a premier Catholic educational apostolate in the United States. They are our strategic partner, providing rich faith formation resources like FORMED, graduate-level theology courses, and collaborative program development to empower our mission in Africa.

Programs & Resources

The conference is open to all lay Catholics seeking deeper theological engagement. It gathers over 200 participants with leading Catholic scholars and ecclesiastical leaders from Africa and the U.S.

FORMED is a digital platform with thousands of Catholic videos, audio talks, and study programs. Starting with a pilot in Abuja, we are rolling FORMED out to Nigerian parishes and schools. To subscribe, you can express interest through our Subscribe.

TSL is a two-year graduate certificate featuring robust online courses from the Augustine Institute, intensive in-person summer courses in Nigeria, and mentorship-based internship placements for cohorts of 25-30 students.
